# Executive Business Case

## Problem
Churn reporting often tells leaders how many customers left, but not where intervention should happen next.

## Business Questions
- Which customer segments churn most?
- Which behaviours correlate with churn?
- How much revenue is currently at risk?
- Which high-value customers should retention teams prioritise?
- Which contract/product groups require commercial review?

## Proposed Action Model
1. Identify high-risk active customers.
2. Rank them by revenue exposure.
3. Separate P1/P2 retention opportunities.
4. Route actions by churn driver:
   - payment failures → billing recovery
   - inactivity / low usage → engagement campaign
   - negative NPS / support load → service recovery
   - month-to-month + high risk → retention offer review
5. Measure retention intervention outcomes.

## Success Measures
- reduced churn rate
- retained annual recurring revenue
- reduced high-risk backlog
- improved NPS / usage
- intervention conversion rate
