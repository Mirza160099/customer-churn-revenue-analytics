# Interview Case Study

## Situation
A business wants to reduce customer churn but lacks a clear view of revenue exposure and which customers should receive retention attention first.

## Task
Create an analytics solution combining churn drivers, segmentation and revenue-at-risk prioritisation.

## Action
- Generated a synthetic customer dataset with contract, product, usage, NPS, support and payment features.
- Created a transparent churn-risk heuristic.
- Calculated annual revenue and revenue at risk.
- Built P1-P4 retention priorities.
- Wrote SQL for segment, contract and behavioural analysis.
- Prepared a Tableau dashboard design for executives and retention teams.
- Documented actions linked to different churn drivers.

## Result
Produced a recruiter-facing business analytics project showing how SQL, Python and Tableau can move from descriptive reporting to prioritised commercial action.

## Interview Talking Points
1. Churn rate vs retention rate.
2. Why correlation is not causation.
3. Revenue at risk vs realised retained revenue.
4. Segment-level churn.
5. Contract effects.
6. NPS and usage signals.
7. How to validate a churn-risk model.
8. How to test retention interventions.
9. Customer lifetime value.
10. How to productionise the pipeline.
