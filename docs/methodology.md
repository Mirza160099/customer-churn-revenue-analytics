# Methodology

## Objective
Identify customer segments and behaviours associated with churn, estimate revenue exposure, and create a retention-priority view.

## Risk Score
The portfolio score uses:
- inactivity
- payment failures
- support demand
- low usage
- negative NPS
- month-to-month contracts

This is a demonstration heuristic, not a production churn model.

## Revenue at Risk
Revenue at risk is estimated using annual customer revenue weighted by the portfolio risk score for active customers.

The estimate is for analytical prioritisation only and is not claimed as guaranteed recoverable revenue.
